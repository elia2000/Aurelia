# Message Menu

**Right-click** a message (on a phone: **press and hold** it) to open its menu. The menu opens from anywhere along the message row, not only on the bubble. From the keyboard, press **Tab** to reach the messages (the list stops on the newest one), move between messages with **Up**, **Down**, **Home** and **End**, and press the **Menu** key (or **Shift+F10**) on the one you want.

The row of seven quick reactions sits above the menu (see [Reactions](./Reactions.md)). Below it, the items always come in this order, and only the ones that make sense for that message are shown:

| Item | What it does | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Copy selected text** | Copies the words you selected with the mouse. | Only when text is selected inside the message. |
| **Reply** | Starts a reply to the message. | Where you can write in the chat. |
| **Copy text** / **Copy link** | Copies the message text, or the link you right-clicked. | Messages with text. |
| **Pin** / **Unpin** | Pins the message under the chat header, or lets it go. See [Pinned Messages and Chats](./Pinned-Messages-and-Chats.md). | Where you may pin: always in direct chats and **Saved messages**, in a group if you own it or the owner allowed members to pin, in a channel if you own it. |
| **Forward** | Sends the message to other chats. | Every message except end-to-end encrypted ones. |
| **Select** | Starts [selecting several messages](./Selecting-Messages.md) with this one ticked. | Every message that can be selected. |
| **Save GIF** | Keeps the GIF in your own [GIF collection](./Saved-GIFs.md). | Only on a GIF, and only when file uploads are on. |
| **Save** | Downloads the message's files. | Messages with files, except end-to-end encrypted ones. |
| **Edit** | Edits your own message. | Your own messages with text. Needs a connection. |
| **Reacted by** | Shows who reacted. | Only where you may see the list: members of direct chats and groups, the owner of a channel. |
| **Delete** | Deletes the message, for you or for everyone. | Always. |

Some messages have a smaller menu:

- A **call entry** ("Outgoing call 0:04") has **Delete** only, and no row of reactions. Deleting it removes it from your own view; the other person keeps theirs. See [Calls & Video Messages](./Calls-and-Video-Messages.md).
- A **service line** ("Bob joined the group", "Alice pinned a message") has no menu. Tapping "pinned a message" jumps to the pinned message.
- Without a connection, **Edit**, **Pin**, **Unpin** and **Save GIF** are greyed out and the quick reactions are hidden.
